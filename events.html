At the heart of Zenith are five major events designed to create valuable opportunities for partner organizations to broaden their reach, strengthen their visibility, and form genuine connections with Senior High School students, BSCS, BSIT, and MMA students. Each event serves as a unique platform for partners to engage with the student community, share their advocacies and expertise, discover potential collaborators, and establish a stronger presence among aspiring technology professionals.

With FEU Alabang ACM’s consistent communication, promotion, coordination, and support throughout the academic year, Zenith goes beyond a traditional event-based partnership by building a shared commitment to growth, meaningful initiatives, and wider community impact. Ultimately, the partnership seeks to create lasting relationships and memorable experiences that will continue to benefit both organizations and the technology community beyond the five major events.

Major event 01

Siklab

Siklab is a two-day institutional technology competition designed to showcase and develop students’ competencies in research, programming, technical knowledge, innovation, problem-solving, and project development. It will feature three major competitions—the Coding Competition, Quiz Bee, and Hackathon—where participants will be challenged to apply their knowledge, think critically, collaborate with their teammates, and create effective solutions to various problems. Through this event, students will be given an opportunity to demonstrate their skills, explore their creativity, gain valuable experience, and strengthen their interest in the field of computing and technology.

PARAGON RECRUIT PHOTO TO BE ADDED

Major event 02

LF: Paragon Recruit

LF: Paragon Recruit is an internal competitive programming competition organized to identify and recruit talented students for the Paragon Team, the school’s official representative in external programming competitions. Through a series of programming challenges, the event will assess participants’ problem-solving, logical reasoning, algorithmic thinking, coding proficiency, and ability to work under pressure. It will also allow students to showcase their skills, gain experience, build confidence, and take their first step toward representing the school in larger competitions.

Major event 03

General Assembly

This activity aims to welcome and orient new members by introducing them to the organization’s activities, opportunities, and plans for the school year. It will also help them understand the organization’s goals, learn how they can actively participate, and discover opportunities for personal and professional growth in computing and technology.

TECH REALITY PHOTO TO BE ADDED

Major event 04

Tech Reality

A fast-paced and engaging seminar that goes beyond traditional academic discussions to present the realities of working in the technology industry. Featuring a prominent tech content creator, app founder, and/or senior engineer, the event will offer honest insights into corporate culture, business challenges, failed ventures, career growth, and the pressures of working in a fast-moving industry. Through personal experiences, practical discussions, and live debugging demonstrations, speakers will provide participants with a more realistic understanding of the skills, mindset, adaptability, and resilience needed to thrive in the tech field.

Major event 05

TechReach (Outreach)

This outreach program is designed to help students from a selected community develop and improve their computing skills through simple, engaging, and hands-on lessons. It aims to build their confidence in using technology, introduce them to essential digital tools, and encourage them to explore the possibilities of computing in their education and future careers.
