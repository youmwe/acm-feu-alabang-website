MISSION

The student chapter anchors itself on the cornerstones of advancing computer science by cultivating and promoting the art, science, engineering, and application of modern computing and information technology in order to encourage and solidify a greater interest in the design, development, construction, languages, management, and applications of fundamental computing concepts.
